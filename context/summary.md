> **0. News check** — direction + does it actually affect this ticker?
> **1.** Vol proof
> **2.** Structure acceptance / rejection (multi-touch or GEX flip hold)
> **3.** 200 EMA premium / discount context
> **4.** GEX regime + flip level (price accepted above/below with volume?)
> **5.** DEX confirm
> **6.** MA20 + MA50 direction on 1H and 4H — trending same way?
> **7.** Entry

## Core Rules (in order of importance)

### 1. News
- News = 3rd vote. Can amplify or veto structure.
- **News aligns** → green light, adds conviction
- **News absent** → structure rules, trade normally
- **News contradicts, rational** → skip, no exceptions
- **News contradicts, irrational / overreaction** → can trade, wider stop, smaller size
- **News sector filter:** ask — does this news actually move this company's fundamentals? Ceasefire doesn't move medical devices. Rate cuts don't move all healthcare. If no → treat as noise.
- **Rejection during a macro pump = extra conviction.** Bulls had tailwind and still couldn't hold. That's real seller strength.

### 2. Stop Placement
- Stop must survive normal price noise, not just look good on paper.
- R:R > 10:1 → automatic red flag. Recalculate. Stop is likely inside noise.
- TSLA min stop = $5+ from entry. Adjust per ticker volatility.
- Wide R:R from tight stop ≠ good trade. It means the stop is fragile.
- Right direction + wrong stop = loss.

### 3. Structure
- Multi-touch acceptance / rejection = high conviction level. 5 touches > 3 > 1.
- Volume spike + multi-touch hold = institutions and dealers both showing their hand.
- Rejection during broad pump = sellers in control even with macro tailwind. Strongest signal.
- Pattern entries (falling wedge etc.) need confirmed break, not mid-pattern entry with tight stop.

### 4. GEX / DEX
- GEX flip = regime pivot. Below flip → trending, dealers amplify. Above → pinning, dealers suppress.
- Entry near GEX flip = coiled setup. Reclaim flip → mechanical dealer buying/selling kicks in.
- **GEX flip acceptance = the real signal.** Price must close and hold above/below flip with volume. A wick that closes back through it is not acceptance.
- DEX + GEX pointing same direction = don't overthink. Trade it.
- Target = next GEX wall. Not arbitrary — that's where dealer hedging creates natural ceiling/floor.
- GEX/DEX reliable in stable environment. Less reliable during active news catalyst on affected tickers.

### 5. 200 EMA
- Price well above 200 EMA = premium. Mean reversion risk. Strengthens shorts.
- Price well below 200 EMA = discount. Bounce risk. Strengthens longs.
- Factor into thesis on all counter-trend trades.

### 6. MA Trend
- MA20 and MA50 trending same direction on 1H and 4H = trend confirmed. Trade with it.
- If MAs are flat or crossed, requires stronger GEX/DEX/volume proof to trade.
- MAs below price and rising = no overhead resistance from averages. Bulls have clean air.
- Five-engine alignment (Vol + GEX flip accept + DEX + MA trend + structure) = maximum conviction. Size accordingly.

### 7. Exit Structure
- Five-engine setup + conviction ≥ 75 = use T1/T2/T3 ladder. Don't collapse to a single target.
- GEX/DEX rehedging sustains moves through multiple levels — the mechanical force doesn't stop at T1.
- Proven ticker + proven setup = priority on next scan. Same mechanics, repeatable outcome.
- Wide stop on high-conviction trade is correct. Don't tighten it out of fear — place it where the thesis actually breaks.
- Confluence maxed + Volume maxed = trade it even with no chart pattern. Those two outweigh pattern absence.

## Evolving Mental Models

**On R:R:**
High R:R is only good if the stop is real. A 20:1 R:R with a $1.47 stop on TSLA is not a 20:1 trade — it's a coin flip with a good-looking number attached.

**On News:**
News is not a binary block or green light. It's a filter with nuance. Direction matters. Sector relevance matters. Rationality matters.

**On Structure:**
Multi-touch levels work because they represent real decisions by real participants. Five touches at a level means five times someone with size committed there. The more touches, the more committed.

**On GEX:**
GEX flip is the most mechanical signal in the system. When price crosses the flip, dealers have to rehedge. That rehedging IS the move. The signal isn't just touching the flip — it's accepting there. Close above/below with volume. That's when the mechanical force fully activates.

**On MA Trend:**
MAs don't predict. They describe. MA20 and MA50 trending up on 1H and 4H means the trend is real across two timeframes. Use it to filter: if MAs confirm trend direction, GEX/DEX are trading with the current.

**On Exit Structure:**
The setup doesn't determine how much you make. The exit structure does. T1/T2/T3 ladder on five-engine setups — the T3 runner is not bonus money, it's the expected move when everything is firing.

**On Repeatable Setups:**
When a ticker has confirmed its response to a setup, that setup earns elevated priority on the next scan. Don't let novelty bias pull you toward unfamiliar tickers when a proven one is giving the same signal.
